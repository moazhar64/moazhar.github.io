Doctoral research scholar · IIT Mandi

Mohammad Azhar U Din Mir

Structural engineering · porous materials · XCT · FEA

I study what's inside materials. Since 2024 I've been a doctoral scholar at IIT Mandi, using X-ray computed tomography to look inside porous materials and building models that link their structure to how they behave under load. Before that I built a ship-hull test tank with Lamb-wave sensors at IIT Delhi, wrote design-automation code at IIT Bombay, and worked on railway track quadrupling sites with GMR. So far: 2 papers, 3 IITs, 10 PhD courses, and 2 courses taught as a TA.
