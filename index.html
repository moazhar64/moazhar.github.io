Research

Porous materials are only as good as their pores. My work connects what we can see inside a material to how it behaves under load.

Solid and fracture mechanics

Deformation, damage and failure in quasi-brittle materials.

Microstructure–property relationships

How pore structure in porous materials controls mechanical response.

Multiscale constitutive modeling

Material models informed by measured microstructure.

How the pieces fit together

Image

X-ray CT reveals a specimen's internal structure without cutting it.

Quantify

Image analysis turns the structure into measurable descriptors.

Model

Those descriptors inform constitutive laws for the material.

Simulate

Finite element analysis predicts how the material and structures respond.
